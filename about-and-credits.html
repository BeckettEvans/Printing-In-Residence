Who made this

Printing in Residence was made by three students in CMNS 390 at the University of the Fraser Valley in Fall 2026, for instructor Kelsey Blair. Beckett Evans wrote Start Here, the Glossary and this page, and built the website. Jade Newton wrote Ask Housing First and Room Setup and Inspection. Bennett wrote the two Get Online pages and Connection Troubleshooting.
